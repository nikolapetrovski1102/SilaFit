## English (U.S.)

Barcode scanning on Pro, AI meal scan on Advanced
- Scan the barcode on a packaged food and it lands in your meal with its calories and macros. Barcode scanning is part of Pro and Advanced.
- Advanced: snap a photo of your meal and AI breaks it into ingredients with portions, calories and macros, 3 meal scans a day.
- Adding a product the catalog doesn't have? Photograph the nutrition label and AI fills in the macros for you.
